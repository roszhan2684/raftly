# Go-to-Market — SalesLens

## Positioning
For sales managers at growing B2B teams who need to coach without listening to every call, SalesLens is a coaching-first
AI assistant that surfaces objections, competitor mentions and coaching moments with the exact quote. Unlike enterprise
conversation-intelligence suites, it's live in a day and priced for mid-market teams.

## Message hierarchy
- Headline: "Coach from the moments that matter, without listening to every call."
- Proof: every insight has the quote and timestamp · set up in under a day (Zoom or upload) · managers clear their queue in 15 minutes a week.
- Objections: "Is the AI accurate?" → evidence quotes + human review + published eval thresholds. "Is it legal to record?" → consent attestation, retention controls; customers remain responsible for their recording notices (legal-reviewed guidance).

## Pricing inputs
Value metric candidates: seats (manager + rep) vs audio hours. Cost to serve scales with audio hours (COST.md). Raftly
recommends per-seat with an included audio-hour allowance (predictable for buyers, protects margin via the allowance).
**The pricing model is an open founder decision**; competitor anchors require dated research before deciding.

## Channels
1. Founder-led outbound to sales leaders at 10–100 rep companies (LinkedIn, warm intros).
2. Content: "objection handling" teardown posts built from anonymized, consented examples.
3. Zoom App Marketplace listing (post-launch; review process to be verified).

## Onboarding and activation
Time-to-first-value target: first insights within 1 hour of the first upload. Activation = ≥ 20 calls ingested and ≥ 3
insights actioned by a manager in week 1. The onboarding checklist in the product drives it.

## Launch assets
Landing page (SEO basics, G-SEO), a demo video with synthetic calls, a security & privacy overview page, the DPA template (after legal review).
