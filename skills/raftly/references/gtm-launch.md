# Go-to-market, launch and technical SEO

Roles: **Head of Product Marketing / GTM** (`gtm`), **Technical SEO Lead** (`seo`), **Launch Director** (`launch-director`).

## Positioning (GTM.md)

- For **<ICP>** who **<need>**, **<product>** is a **<category>** that **<key benefit>**. Unlike
  **<alternative>**, it **<differentiator>**.
- Message hierarchy: one headline promise, three supporting proof points, objections and answers.
- Keep claims honest and evidenced. No "secure", "compliant" or "AI-powered accuracy" claims without evidence.

## Pricing inputs (founder decides pricing)

Raftly supplies the inputs: the value metric candidates (seats, usage, outcomes), the cost-to-serve per unit
(AI/transcription cost matters), competitor price anchors (researched and dated), packaging options and
their implications for billing architecture (per-seat vs usage changes metering, entitlements and invoices).
The pricing decision itself is a FOUNDER decision. Changing it later runs the Requirement Change Engine.

## Channels and onboarding

Pick 1–2 channels that fit the ICP (communities, content/SEO, outbound, app stores, integrations
marketplaces). Design onboarding around time-to-first-value: the first moment the core job succeeds.
Define the activation metric.

## Launch plan (LAUNCH.md)

sequencing (private beta → public), readiness gate results (production-gate.md), rollback plan, owners
and support path, announcement assets, success metrics for the launch window, and the remaining accepted risks.

## Technical SEO (public sites only)

Crawlable server-rendered or static HTML for public pages; unique titles and meta descriptions; canonical
URLs; `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt`; structured data where it fits (e.g. SoftwareApplication, FAQ);
Open Graph and social images; Core Web Vitals within budget; fast, accessible landing pages; no indexing of
app or authenticated routes. For a calculator website, the public page *is* the product, so performance and
accessibility are the SEO.
